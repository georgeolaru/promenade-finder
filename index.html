Where does a place come alive?

Type any city, town or village and PromenadeFinder locates its main promenade — the pedestrian streets, squares, waterfronts and boulevards where people stroll, socialize and soak up the local atmosphere.

It works by analyzing OpenStreetMap: pedestrian infrastructure is scored together with the density of cafés, restaurants and public spaces, then clustered into distinct areas and ranked. Some places genuinely have no promenade — the app will tell you that too. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors · geocoding by Nominatim · queries via Overpass API. Results reflect what is mapped in OSM; under-mapped villages may show fewer features than reality.
